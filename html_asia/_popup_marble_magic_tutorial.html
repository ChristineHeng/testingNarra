What is Marble Magic?
  • A racing game where marbles compete on different tracks.
  • Each race has unique obstacles, traps, and formats that affect the outcome.
How do I play?
  • Choose your bet type: Winner Time Over/Under, Head-to-Head, etc.
  • Check the race stats and recent results before placing your bet.
Tips for New Players
  • Start with simple bets like Winner Time Over/Under.
  • Use past results and stats to spot patterns.
Multiple Track Formats
S8
Solo 8
8 marbles race individually. Includes both pre-race and live race, with either 1 or 2 laps.
S6
Solo 6
6 marbles race individually. Includes pre-race and live race, with 1 lap only.
S5
Solo 5
5 marbles race individually. Includes pre-race and live race, with 1 lap only.
1v1
1 vs 1
2 marbles compete individually in a head-to-head format. Pre-race only, with 1 lap.
3v2
3 vs 2
5 marbles compete in a 3 vs 2 team format. Includes pre-race and live race, with 1 lap only.
3v3
3 vs 3
6 marbles compete in teams of 3. Pre-race only, with 1 lap only.
